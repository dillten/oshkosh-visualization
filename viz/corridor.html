The route

Five checkpoints, one railroad track.

Inbound VFR traffic funnels through Endeavor Bridge, Puckaway Lake and Green Lake, then joins the railroad tracks at Ripon for the final run to Fisk — the point where Fisk approach takes over on the radio. Distances are measured along that published route, not straight-line.
